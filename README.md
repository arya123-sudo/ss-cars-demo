# SS Cars — Demo Website

A single-page demo website (`index.html`, self-contained, no build step) built for **SS Cars**, a used-car showroom in Yelahanka, Bengaluru, as part of Arya's website + AI video agency work.

- The cars, prices and stock shown on the page are **sample placeholders**, clearly labelled on the page itself — they are to be replaced with the showroom's actual stock, photos and prices once the owner confirms them.
- The rating shown (5.0★ from 399 Justdial ratings) is from the showroom's public Justdial listing, researched 31 Oct 2026.
- Every car on the page has a WhatsApp enquiry button, so buyers can ask about a specific car directly instead of through a listing portal.
- Photos are royalty-free Pexels stock images, credited in the page footer.

**No one at the showroom has been contacted about this demo yet.**
